About Navarre Web Care

A local business, built for local businesses.

Navarre Web Care is a small, newly established web studio based right here in Navarre, Florida. We started with a simple observation: too many excellent local businesses are hard to find online — or their only web presence lives on a social platform they don't own and can't control.

Our work is giving Navarre-area businesses a home of their own on the internet — a clean, professional website where customers can find you, trust what they see, and reach you in one tap. Every project is handled personally by the person you first spoke with, priced plainly, and delivered quickly, because that's how we'd want it done for us.

Websites are the heart of what we do, but not the whole of it. We also provide social media marketing for businesses that want their message working across every channel, and ongoing care for existing websites — updates, security, and small monthly changes handled quietly in the background, even if we didn't build the site.

WE HANDLE EVERYTHING YOU WONT HAVE TO WORRY ABOUT DOMAIN, HOSTING OR SEOs

Why are our prices lower than the agencies'? Because you're not funding a team of ten people who each vaguely touch your project. Navarre Web Care is one full-stack developer who builds your site end to end — the pages your customers see, the forms and databases working behind them, and the servers it all runs on. Building that kind of infrastructure is the hobby that became this business. Fewer hands means lower overhead, tighter security, and one person who genuinely knows how every piece of your website works.

If you're a local business thinking about your first website — or tired of wrestling with the one you have — we'd love to talk. It's a fifteen-minute conversation with a neighbor, not a sales pitch.
